## Findings and edits

### Unit 21 — reflexives

- Changed the lexicon entry `voelen=sentire` to `zich voelen=sentirsi`, matching the construction actually taught and tested.
- Explained full and reduced reflexive forms, polite `u/zich`, and the less usual but valid reflexive `jullie`.
- Added accepted `mij`, `mezelf/mijzelf`, `onszelf`, and `uzelf/zichzelf` fill variants where appropriate. Added `wij/we`, `zij/ze`, valid emphatic reflexives, and grammatical object/predicate fronting to sentence answers.
- Distinguished optional reflexive uses of `wassen/aankleden` from obligatory constructions such as `zich vergissen`. Did not mechanically add `-zelf` to every reflexive verb or front weak `me/je/zich`.
- Replaced the overly literal suggestion that all reflexives describe an action performed on oneself: `zich voelen` expresses a state. Added the body-part construction `ik was mijn handen`.
- Lexical phrases beginning with `zich` remain content entries, distinct from the keyword `zich`. Translation distractors `haasten`, `voelen`, and `vergissen` match those entries after stripping the reflexive prefix.

The distinction between ordinary and emphatic reflexives, including the strong form required in first position, was checked against [Team Taaladvies: zich/zichzelf](https://www.vlaanderen.be/team-taaladvies/taaladviezen/zich-zichzelf), [E-ANS: optional reflexive constructions](https://e-ans.ivdnt.org/topics/pid/ans0503040302lingtopic), and [Team Taaladvies: uzelf/zichzelf](https://www.vlaanderen.be/team-taaladvies/taaladviezen/uzelf-zichzelf).

### Unit 22 — diminutives

- Added the missing adjective `groen`.
- Expanded the lexicon to 14 with `kop`, `boom`, `ring`, `koning`, and `meisje`. This explicitly teaches the bases needed for the diminutive exercises, instead of relying on an unexplained base form.
- Added the lexicalized example `het meisje`, including the Italian meanings and the distinction between grammatical neuter gender and a female referent.
- Retained correct `ringetje/koninkje`, neuter singular articles, plural `-s`, and uninflected `klein` after indefinite `een`.
- Added valid `we` and fronted constituent alternatives. The Italian translations continue to distinguish definite/indefinite articles and near/far demonstratives.

### Unit 23 — er

- Added `liggen` and `staan` to the lexicon and explained their present-tense forms before testing `ligt/staan`. Explained conventional position as well as horizontal/vertical orientation.
- Added `nog`, used in the title, to the keywords and explanation.
- Corrected the translation distractor in `23-m2` from `plannen` to exact own-unit bare lexicon form `plan`.
- Removed invalid adjacent spellings `er op` and `er aan` from accepted pronominal-adverb answers. Adjacent parts are written `erop/eraan`; actual separation needs intervening material. Changed `23-m6` to `Wij denken er vandaag aan`, with matching Italian and valid joined alternatives.
- Explicitly introduced `wachten op`, `denken aan`, and `rekenen op`, rather than leaving their prepositions implicit.
- Added valid quantity/predicate fronting and `wij/we` alternatives. Retained locative `daar` as a valid, more emphatic alternative to `er`.
- Tightened `23-g1` and `23-g5` to request the verb “to be,” avoiding competition from newly taught positional verbs.

Spelling was verified against [Team Taaladvies: erop/er op](https://www.vlaanderen.be/team-taaladvies/taaladviezen/erop-er-op), [eraan/er aan](https://www.vlaanderen.be/team-taaladvies/taaladviezen/eraan-er-aan), and [Van Dale's exact rekenen example](https://www.vandale.nl/blogs/taaladvies/wat-is-juist-ik-reken-erop-of-ik-reken-er-op).

### Unit 24 — prepositions

- Introduced spatial `over` explicitly, separate from its previously taught clock-time meaning, and introduced `zonder` with an example.
- Added those two keyword entries in place of already introduced `in/naar`; the explanation still teaches both of the latter.
- Distinguished `denken aan` in the sense of having something in mind from active reflection with `denken over`. Tightened the Italian and hint in `24-g5` to specify the intended sense. This avoids treating every alternative preposition as inherently ungrammatical. See [Taaladvies: denken aan/over](https://taaladvies.net/denken-aan-of-over/).
- Changed the bridge sentence's Italian to `L'autobus attraversa il ponte` and aligned `wandelen` with the previously taught `passeggiare`.
- Added valid prepositional-phrase fronting and `wij/we` variants. Retained the distinctions among location, destination, origin, clock time, and companionship.

### Unit 25 — infinitives

- Added missing `volgen=seguire`.
- Explained optional `om` in relevant infinitival complements, without presenting it as universally insertable after every verb. Kept `beginnen te lezen` and the neutral `hopen te komen` construction.
- Reframed `25-g1` as `Ik probeer om ___ lezen`, explicitly requesting one word. The earlier blank could legitimately be completed with either `te` or the two-word `om te`.
- Added optional-`om` answers with `besluiten`, `vergeten`, `beloven`, and `proberen`, and appropriate alternatives for `moeilijk (om) te volgen`.
- Added fronted purpose clauses and the voluntary-motion construction `komen + bare infinitive`, such as `Hij komt werken`. These are not a general licence to delete `te`.
- Completed the reflexive explanation example with an object: `ik probeer me die dag te herinneren`, with Italian translation.

Checked against [Team Taaladvies: proberen](https://www.vlaanderen.be/team-taaladvies/taaladviezen/proberen-om-proberen-van), [E-ANS: om in infinitival clauses](https://e-ans.ivdnt.org/topics/pid/ans190303lingtopic), [E-ANS: komen plus bare infinitive](https://e-ans.ivdnt.org/topics/pid/ans180504030401lingtopic), and [E-ANS: adjective complements](https://e-ans.ivdnt.org/topics/pid/ans150403lingtopic).

Vocabulary progression was checked against the current earlier units, including explanations, and against each unit's own entries. Remaining newly encountered surface forms are inflections or explicitly taught forms, not missing content lemmas: for example `handen/gewassen`, `boekjes/ringen`, `plaatsen/vragen`, and `belooft/besluit/vergeet`. Earlier introductions include everyday objects and pronouns, time words, adjectives, `wandelen/rijden`, separable verbs, and `proberen`.

Regional notes remain conservative. The informal Belgian register of `goesting` is supported by the linked [Canon van Vlaanderen article](https://www.canonvanvlaanderen.be/events/goesting/); the Belgian-standard status of `om hoe laat` by [Team Taaladvies](https://www.vlaanderen.be/team-taaladvies/taaladviezen/hoe-laat-om-hoe-laat-om-welk-uur); and the general standard-language distinctions by [Taaladvies](https://taaladvies.net/wat-is-standaardtaal-algemeen/).

## Verification and limits

- Ten grammar and ten mixed questions per unit; one blank per grammar item; nonempty Italian, hints, and notes; three distinct MC options with the designated answer present.
- Single-token accepted fill answers, unique normalized answer strings, and all accepted mixed answers constructible from the non-distractor tile multiset, including repeated tokens.
- All 25 designated order distractors muted (`!` produces `speak:false`).
- All 25 translation distractors spoken real words and exact members of their own unit's content lexicon after stripping `de`, `het`, or lexical `zich`.
- Unique content entries, no normalized keyword/content collision, all vocabulary limits, and the targeted regressions described above.
- Scoped `git diff --check`: passed; only routine Windows LF/CRLF notices.

This is a content/data review, not a browser, speech, accessibility, or full-course build test. It relies on the stated runtime contract that hints and full Italian are visible and that answer variants are accepted. Tile checks validate the helper's generated data, not visual layout; the 14-word block arrangement was not rendered. No helper/runtime change was made to implement article/reflexive normalization.

Fronted and emphatic answers are accepted grammatical variants, not claims that all alternatives are equally neutral. Finite answer lists do not enumerate every possible paraphrase or discourse-dependent construction. Vocabulary progression is a snapshot: concurrent changes to earlier units or later runtime integration require a fresh integration check.
